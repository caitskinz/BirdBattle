Pecking Order Terms and Conditions

This card entitles the bearer to sit at the top of the pecking order for 12 months from the date of hatching (issue).

The bearer may cut in line once per day at the coffee machine, microwave or printer. Colleagues must step aside.

The bearer gets first pick of all shared baked goods, biscuit tins and morning tea platters. Crumbs are to be left for the rest of the flock.

The bearer may claim the best chair in any meeting room by simply perching on it.

The bearer’s opinion on where to go for team lunch outranks all others, unless the restaurant serves chicken, in which case the motion is void on ethical grounds.

Other staff may not ruffle the bearer’s feathers. Any ruffling must be immediately smoothed with a compliment or a snack.

The privilege card is non-transferable, non-refundable and may not be exchanged for cash, worms or seeds.

The bearer may not use their status to override the CEO, fire alarms, health and safety procedures, or the Operations Manager’s calendar.

Loss of card must be reported immediately. A replacement may be issued, but the bearer will be demoted to “pigeon” until it arrives.

Pecking order rights expire at the crowning of next year’s champion, at which point the bearer must gracefully return to the nest like everyone else.
